# ADR 0010: Research is evidence-led and local-first

Status: Accepted

## Decision

Research follows bounded request, plan, retrieval, read, dedupe,
cross-check, synthesis, citation validation, and report stages. Evidence is a
bounded excerpt with locator and fingerprint. Retrieved instructions are
untrusted data and never executable policy.

## Consequences

Offline local documents remain useful without a hosted search or model. Browser
and SearXNG are injected optional providers. A future durable evidence store
must preserve owner binding, provenance, limits, and citation links.
